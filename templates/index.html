Amar Tools

Generators for the Amar RPG: people and monsters with full 3-tier stats, encounters, towns with their inhabitants, weather and names. Pick one in the menu.

The same tools run in a terminal too: see Amar Tools on GitHub.

On a keyboard, the arrow keys or j and k move in the menu, Enter picks, Tab jumps to the form and Escape goes back.
